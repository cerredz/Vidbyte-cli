# Feedback

## Description
A feedback suggestion seeks a specific reaction from someone positioned to notice what the caller cannot. It names what to show, who should see it, and what question to ask. Useful feedback is early enough to change the work and developed enough to produce an informed response. The suggestion should distinguish signal from general approval. It should explain which possible reactions would alter the next step. Feedback is an information-gathering action, not a substitute for making the caller's own decision.

## Things to consider
- Which blind spot needs another perspective?
- Who has relevant experience or standing?
- What artifact or question should be shared?
- What response would be actionable?
- Is the work ready for useful feedback?
- Is there still time to revise cheaply?
- How should conflicting reactions be weighed?
- What decision will the feedback influence?
