# Quick Wins

## Description
A quick-win suggestion offers a small, inexpensive action with a useful near-term payoff. It can sit outside the main plan when its value is clear and its distraction cost is low. The benefit should be concrete rather than merely pleasant. The action should not depend on a long chain of uncertain follow-up work. A quick win can build momentum, reduce friction, or create evidence for a larger choice. Its bounded nature is what makes the opportunity worth taking soon.

## Things to consider
- What small action has a clear payoff?
- How much time, attention, or money does it require?
- What friction or uncertainty does it remove?
- Could it distract from a higher priority?
- Does it create a durable benefit or only activity?
- What dependencies are absent or minimal?
- How will the payoff be observed?
- What is the stopping point if the benefit does not appear?
