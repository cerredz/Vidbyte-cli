# Long-Term Suggestions

## Purpose

Develop durable directions that connect present work to a longer planning horizon. A long-term suggestion should create a capability, asset, relationship, or position that compounds beyond the current task. It must distinguish a practical path over 3-6 months from a larger direction over 2 years or more. It should help the caller decide what to build toward without pretending that a distant forecast is certain.

## Intent

Describe the durable outcome, the reason it matters, and the milestones that connect today's state to that outcome. Separate the near-term capability-building path from the longer-range direction, and state which assumptions must remain true for the connection to hold.

## Timing

Use this category when the caller is choosing what to cultivate over a 3-6 month horizon or what durable direction to pursue over a 2 year+ horizon. A strong suggestion may cover both horizons, but it must show how the 3-6 month milestone tests or enables the 2 year+ direction.

## Checks

- Name the 3-6 month capability, milestone, or evidence target and the 2 year+ direction it supports.
- Identify the next planning checkpoint, the durable benefit, and the signal that would justify changing course.

## Cautions

- Do not use this category for an ordinary next step that belongs in continuation or immediate execution.
- Do not present a speculative 2 year+ vision without a credible 3-6 month probe or milestone.
