# Stop or Defer

## Description
A stop-or-defer suggestion protects attention from work whose present value does not justify its cost. Stopping ends a direction when the case against continuation is strong enough to accept the lost option. Deferral preserves the option while naming the condition that would make it worth revisiting. The recommendation should explain what more valuable work is displaced by continuing. It should preserve enough state for a responsible restart when appropriate. Use this category when changing allocation is more valuable than forcing progress on the current direction.

## Why use / use cases
- [ ] **The expected value has fallen below the cost.** New evidence may show that the work will not repay its time, money, or attention. Use this category to stop or defer with the reason and threshold visible.
- [ ] **A higher-value commitment is being crowded out.** Continuing may consume the scarce resource needed elsewhere. Suggest a stop or deferral when the displaced work has a clearer consequence or benefit.
- [ ] **A dependency will not arrive soon enough.** Waiting actively may create repeated context switching or idle effort. Use deferral when the state can be preserved and a real trigger determines when to resume.
- [ ] **The direction no longer fits the goal.** A changed outcome, market, constraint, or owner may make prior work less relevant. Recommend stopping when continuing would serve a commitment the caller no longer endorses.
- [ ] **A promising idea needs better timing.** The idea may remain valuable but not under current capacity or conditions. Use deferral when preserving the option costs less than pursuing it now.
- [ ] **More effort is unlikely to resolve the core issue.** Repeated attempts may be accumulating sunk cost without new evidence. Suggest stopping when no credible intervention or learning path remains.
- [ ] **The work is being continued only because it has started.** Existing effort can create pressure to finish a low-value direction. Use this category to separate sunk cost from the value of future work.
- [ ] **A clean pause would reduce harm.** Continuing may increase risk, confusion, or stakeholder burden while a pause allows conditions to stabilize. Recommend deferral when the restart state and owner can be maintained.

## Things to consider
- What exact work or direction should stop or be deferred?
- What evidence makes its present value insufficient?
- What higher-value outcome or commitment would receive the released capacity?
- Is the correct decision a permanent stop, a timed deferral, or a conditional pause?
- What option value would be lost by stopping?
- What state, rationale, artifacts, and owner must be preserved for restart?
- What trigger, date, or evidence would make reconsideration worthwhile?
- Who needs to know the decision and what ambiguity must be removed?

## When not to use
Do not use this category merely because the work is difficult, uncertain, or temporarily blocked when its value and direction remain sound. Avoid it when the next step is small and required for completion or when a prerequisite can be obtained promptly. If competing commitments need to be ranked, use prioritization; if the work needs a safer smaller test, use experiment.
