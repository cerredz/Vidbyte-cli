# Stop or Defer

## Description
A stop-or-defer suggestion recommends ending or postponing work whose present value does not justify its cost. It includes a reason and a condition for reconsideration.

## Goal
Protect attention and resources from low-value, premature, blocked, or obsolete work.

## Intent
Use this type when continuing would displace more valuable progress or deepen unrecoverable commitment.

## Timeline
Stopping applies immediately when the case is decisive. Deferral names a later trigger, date, dependency, or evidence threshold.

## Checklist
- Explain the displacement cost.
- Separate stopping from waiting.
- Define the reconsideration condition.

## Cautions
Do not use deferral to hide uncertainty or avoidance. Preserve any state needed for a responsible restart.
