# Coordination

## Description
A coordination suggestion resolves alignment between people, agents, or dependent workstreams. It turns hidden ownership, sequencing, or communication gaps into explicit commitments. The recommendation should name the decision or exchange that unlocks progress. It should prefer a clear written boundary when a meeting would add little value. Shared responsibility is not enough without an accountable decision maker. The result should make the next handoff and its confirmation visible.

## Things to consider
- Which actors or workstreams are interdependent?
- What ownership is unclear?
- What decision controls sequencing?
- What information must cross the boundary?
- Who is accountable for the next choice?
- What is the smallest useful exchange?
- How will receipt or agreement be confirmed?
- What happens if coordination fails or is delayed?
