# Long-Term Directions

## Description
A long-term-direction suggestion identifies a capability, body of work, market, or relationship worth developing over an extended period. It is broader than the next task but still connected to a meaningful goal. The direction should acknowledge uncertainty and the investment it would require. It should offer a near-term signal that can test whether continued pursuit is sensible. Long-term value does not excuse vague ambition. The suggestion should show how present choices can preserve or create the future option.

## Things to consider
- What future outcome makes the direction valuable?
- Which capability or asset would compound over time?
- What investment and opportunity cost are involved?
- What assumptions are most uncertain?
- What early signal would justify continuing?
- What can be tested before commitment grows?
- Which options should remain open?
- When should the direction be reviewed?
