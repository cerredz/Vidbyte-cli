# Prioritization

## Description
A prioritization suggestion helps the caller choose among competing goals, tasks, or commitments. It makes the tradeoff explicit, including what will receive less attention as a result. The choice should consider urgency, consequence, reversibility, and the cost of delay. A useful priority reflects the caller's stated values rather than the loudest request. It should identify a small next commitment that makes the choice real. Prioritization is complete only when displaced work and revisit conditions are visible.

## Things to consider
- Which commitments are genuinely competing?
- What consequence follows from delay?
- Which choice is hardest to reverse?
- What value or goal should govern the tradeoff?
- What will receive less attention?
- What evidence could change the order?
- What is the smallest first commitment?
- When should the priority be reviewed?
