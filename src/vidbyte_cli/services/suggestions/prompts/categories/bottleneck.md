# Bottleneck

## Description
A bottleneck removes a constraint that limits throughput, quality, or learning. It targets the narrow point governing the larger system's pace.

## Goal
Increase useful flow by relieving the constraint with the highest measurable downstream effect.

## Intent
Use this type when one wait, resource, process, or decision dominates overall progress.

## Timeline
Address active bottlenecks now when they block several tasks. Later work is appropriate when the constraint has not yet become binding.

## Checklist
- Identify the constrained flow.
- Measure or bound the limiting effect.
- Explain what improves after relief.

## Cautions
Do not optimize a visible inconvenience that is not rate-limiting. Watch for moving the bottleneck elsewhere.
