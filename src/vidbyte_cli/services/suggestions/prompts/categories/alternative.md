# Alternative

## Purpose

Compare a materially different route to the same goal. An alternative should change an important mechanism, sequence, owner, or resource assumption rather than rename the current plan. It gives the caller a real choice with visible tradeoffs. The comparison should preserve the goal while exposing a different path to it.

## Intent

Describe the current route, the distinct route, and the differences in cost, risk, speed, quality, and reversibility. Recommend a first comparison step that does not require committing to the alternative.

## Timing

Use this category when the current route has meaningful weaknesses or when a decision point makes route choice valuable. Do not reopen a settled route without a concrete reason.

## Checks

- Identify the changed assumption that makes the route materially different.
- Explain what evidence would make the caller choose the alternative or keep the current path.

## Cautions

- Do not present a minor optimization as a separate route.
- Do not create choice overload when the current route is adequate and reversible.
