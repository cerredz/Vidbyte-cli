# Completion

## Description
A completion suggestion closes a specific obligation that remains inside the current task. It is concerned with reaching a recognizable endpoint, not adding polish around finished work. The missing piece may be a decision, verification, artifact, communication, or handoff. The suggestion should connect that piece to the outcome the caller originally wanted. Its completion signal must be inspectable by someone who did not perform the work. A good completion suggestion narrows attention until the existing commitment can honestly be called done.

## Things to consider
- What obligation is demonstrably unfinished?
- Why does the current state not prove completion?
- Which requirement or promise does the gap affect?
- What exact artifact or observation will close it?
- Who needs to recognize or accept the result?
- What dependency could prevent closure?
- Which optional improvements should remain outside scope?
- What final check protects against a false completion claim?
