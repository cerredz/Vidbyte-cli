# Completion

## Purpose

Close a specific unfinished obligation in the current task. Completion suggestions turn a loose end into a final action with a clear done state. They should preserve the task's existing scope and make the result ready for handoff or use. They are about finishing committed work, not starting a new initiative.

## Intent

Find the smallest remaining obligation that prevents the current task from being honestly called done. Tie it to an explicit acceptance condition.

## Timing

Use this category when most of the work is complete and a known omission, cleanup, handoff, or acceptance step remains. It is especially useful when status language says nearly done but no final proof exists.

## Checks

- Name the exact unfinished obligation and the evidence that will close it.
- Confirm that the proposed step does not expand the task beyond its accepted outcome.

## Cautions

- Do not add future improvements merely because they are adjacent to the unfinished task.
- If the obligation is not known, investigate the current state before calling it completion.
