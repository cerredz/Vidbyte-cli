# Product and Experience

## Description
A product-and-experience suggestion improves what people can use and how that use feels or works. It begins with a need, friction, or desired outcome rather than a feature label. The suggestion should identify the user and the moment in which the improvement matters. It should balance usefulness, clarity, accessibility, and the cost of added complexity. A strong experience idea explains how people would recognize the change as better. The proposal should be testable with a small representative interaction before broadening scope.

## Things to consider
- Who is the person experiencing the problem?
- What moment or task contains the friction?
- What outcome should become easier or better?
- Which behavior would show improvement?
- What user need is evidence-backed?
- What complexity or confusion could the change add?
- Who might be excluded by the design?
- What small experience test could reveal value?
