# Strategy

## Purpose

Choose a broader direction that coordinates several actions toward the goal. A strategy suggestion changes priorities, sequencing, positioning, or resource allocation at a meaningful level. It should explain why the direction is worth choosing and what it displaces. It must still identify a first probe or commitment that makes the direction actionable.

## Intent

Connect a directional choice to the goal, the opportunity it creates, the costs it imposes, and the alternatives it leaves aside. Keep the direction broad enough to coordinate work but concrete enough to evaluate.

## Timing

Use this category when several next actions depend on a shared direction or when the current path's assumptions need a deliberate choice. It is appropriate before a cluster of execution decisions, not for every small task.

## Checks

- State the decision, the relevant tradeoff, and the first evidence or milestone.
- Explain the opportunity cost and the condition that would trigger a strategic change.

## Cautions

- Do not use strategy to avoid naming an available immediate action.
- For a multi-horizon direction with explicit 3-6 month and 2 year+ milestones, use long-term suggestions.
