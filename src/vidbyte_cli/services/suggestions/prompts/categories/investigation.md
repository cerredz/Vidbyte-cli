# Investigation

## Description
An investigation gathers missing knowledge before action or decision. It follows an explicit question and stops when additional research has low decision value.

## Goal
Acquire the smallest body of reliable evidence needed to resolve a named uncertainty.

## Intent
Use this type when observation or authoritative sources are more appropriate than intervention.

## Timeline
Investigate before dependent commitments. Time-box exploration so research does not become indefinite avoidance.

## Checklist
- State the unresolved question.
- Name the cheapest authoritative sources.
- Define a stopping and synthesis rule.

## Cautions
Do not collect information without a decision connection. Distinguish source authority from mere volume.
