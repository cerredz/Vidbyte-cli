# Investigation

## Description
An investigation gathers missing knowledge before a consequential action or decision. It begins with a question and ends when enough reliable evidence is available. The work should prefer sources that are authoritative, accessible, and relevant to the choice at hand. More information is not automatically better if it cannot change the decision. A useful investigation includes a stopping point and a way to synthesize what was learned. It keeps research from becoming an attractive substitute for commitment.

## Things to consider
- What precise question remains unresolved?
- Which decision depends on its answer?
- What source is most authoritative and affordable?
- Which evidence would be sufficient?
- How should conflicting sources be handled?
- What should be ignored as low decision value?
- What is the stopping rule for further inquiry?
- How will the findings change the next action?
