# Adjacent Opportunity

## Purpose

Capture nearby value enabled by current work without abandoning the stated goal. An adjacent opportunity shares a capability, audience, asset, or timing advantage with the main effort. It is close enough to be cheaper now than later, but distinct enough to be more than routine completion. The suggestion should make the connection and the boundary visible.

## Intent

Identify the enabling work already underway, the nearby opportunity it unlocks, and the smallest probe that tests whether the opportunity is worth pursuing. Keep the opportunity subordinate to the primary goal until evidence supports expansion.

## Timing

Use this category when current work creates a credible low-cost opening that may disappear or become more expensive if ignored. Prefer it after the enabling asset or signal exists, not before.

## Checks

- Name the shared asset, capability, audience, or timing advantage.
- Explain the opportunity cost and the trigger for pursuing it without derailing the main work.

## Cautions

- Do not use this category for unrelated novelty or a broad strategic bet.
- If the opportunity requires a separate long-range investment, use strategy or long-term suggestions.
