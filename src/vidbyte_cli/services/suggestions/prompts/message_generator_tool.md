Sends one message to the generator agent, the agent that wrote the slate you are reviewing, and stops your review immediately. The generator keeps one conversation for the whole run, and your message is placed in that conversation as its next turn, so it reads your words directly before it revises the slate. Call this tool only when one finding outweighs a full graded review, such as a slate built on a misreading of the goal, evidence that contradicts most candidates, or a constraint the whole slate violates. Write the message so it stands on its own, naming the problem, the candidate identifiers and context references that show it, and what a repaired slate must do differently. The moment the tool records your message, your turn ends, and no structured review is returned, so anything you meant to say must be in the message itself. The number of times critics may message the generator in one run is capped by the caller, and this tool is withheld once that cap is spent. Do not use it for routine feedback that belongs in your normal structured review.
