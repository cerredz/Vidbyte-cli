In-progress work identifies activity already underway and the territory it occupies. Clear ownership and outcome wording help the agent avoid creating duplicate parallel effort.

Generation can propose support, verification, or an alternative only when it does not merely restate the active work. Critique weighs overlap before ranking a candidate.

Omission means active-work context was not provided and does not imply inactivity. A statement also marked complete is preserved as a contradiction for the caller to resolve.
