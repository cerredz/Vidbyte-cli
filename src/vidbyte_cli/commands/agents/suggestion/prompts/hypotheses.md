Hypotheses are unverified beliefs paired with an explicit confidence level. Each entry should distinguish what is believed from how strongly the available evidence supports it.

The generator can propose investigations or experiments that confirm or refute consequential beliefs. Ideas depending on low-confidence hypotheses must surface that dependency instead of presenting it as fact.

A hypothesis is neither evidence nor a settled decision. Omission means no belief inventory was supplied and leaves the agent to label inferred assumptions conservatively.
