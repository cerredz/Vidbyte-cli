Risks identify known fragile areas and the failure modes associated with them. A useful entry explains what could break, what makes the area vulnerable, and how the failure would be recognized.

The critic requires a mitigation or verification step for candidates that touch a named risk. Unsupported exposure lowers readiness even when the underlying idea remains valuable.

Risk context supports prevention without treating every possibility as a blocker. Omission means no risk register was supplied and does not establish that the plan is safe.
