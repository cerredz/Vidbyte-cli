Blockers describe work that cannot progress and the condition or party it is waiting on. A useful entry names both the stuck outcome and the dependency preventing movement.

The generator looks for ways to remove, route around, or productively wait on the blocker. Candidates that still depend on the unresolved condition are marked for a later horizon and blocked readiness.

Blockers are current state rather than permanent constraints. Omission means no blocker information was supplied and does not prove the work is unblocked.
