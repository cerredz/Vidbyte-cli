Outcomes describe what happened after an earlier suggestion was actually carried out. They should capture both intended results and consequential side effects, regressions, or newly exposed needs.

The generator uses outcomes to propose follow-up work instead of merely suppressing old ideas. The critic checks whether a candidate responds to the observed result rather than repeating the original action.

Outcome history closes the feedback loop left open by a prior-suggestions record. Omission means execution results were not supplied, not that prior suggestions had no effects.
