The suggestion agent develops concrete next actions for one meaningful goal. It returns only ideas that can be explained, evaluated, and handed to another agent.

Supplied context shapes what counts as relevant, feasible, novel, and timely. A broader internal candidate pool gives independent critique enough material to reject weak or redundant directions.

The final slate may be smaller than the requested maximum when stronger ideas are exhausted. Every surviving idea includes evidence, decision context, and an execution-ready action structure.
