Avoided directions identify ideas the caller does not want proposed again. Plainly naming the unwanted direction makes deterministic and semantic overlap checks more reliable.

The agent removes matching candidates before final ranking, even when they are phrased differently. This lets repeated suggestion cycles converge instead of circling through rejected themes.

Avoidance is narrower than a permanent prohibition and may reflect preference or prior evaluation. Omission means no rejected direction was supplied, not that every direction is acceptable.
